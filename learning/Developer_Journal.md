Principle #1

Information over opinions.

We explain why we reached a conclusion.

Principle #2

Show the evidence.

If EdgeIQ recommends a player, users should be able to click and see the supporting data.

Principle #3

Personalized first.

Two users with different fantasy leagues, favorite teams, or betting interests shouldn't see the same dashboard.

Principle #4

Save users time.

If we can turn two hours of research into five minutes without hiding the reasoning, we've created real value.

Principle #5

Keep it understandable.

Our users shouldn't need a statistics degree to benefit from the platform.

If EdgeIQ says:

"Josh Allen's projection increased by 2.4 points."

The user should be able to click Why? and immediately understand the reason in plain English.

Something like:

"His opponent's starting cornerback has been ruled out, and the weather forecast improved. Those two changes increased his projected passing efficiency."

I think that's a huge opportunity. There are plenty of analytics sites that overwhelm people with numbers. We can make advanced analysis approachable.
